# Storyboard: No es decoración. Es entrega.

**Format:** 1080x1920, 9:16, 30 fps
**Duration:** 15.00 seconds, with narration ending at 14.56s
**Audio:** HyperFrames local Spanish TTS (`em_alex`, speed 1.2) + original local paper/shutter/stamp SFX
**VO direction:** Assured Spanish voice, professional and direct, slightly faster than conversational
**Style basis:** `DESIGN.md` and the captured GoDigital Delivery Dossier site
**Rhythm:** fast-fast-SLOW-fast (`hook-PUNCH-hold-CTA`)
**Motion thesis:** Document review becomes delivery proof; one red correction line evolves into a final approval stamp.

## Beat 1: Redline the Wrong Brief (0.00-2.54s)

**VO:** “Una web bonita no pone tu negocio en marcha.”

**Concept:** We enter mid-review. “WEB BONITA” looks polished for a fraction of a second, then a redline rejects it and the real GoDigital dossier takes over. The contradiction lands visually, verbally, and typographically before one second.

**Visual:** A cobalt blueprint field fills the canvas. A moving crop of `scroll-000.png` anchors the lower depth plane. A bright paper sheet drops over it with `WEB BONITA`, a black redaction bar, review metadata, crop marks, and a clipped GoDigital logo. The red review line draws through the phrase while the corrected on-screen line occupies most of the left/top zone. A dossier edge rises from the bottom and becomes the next scene.

**Camera:** Fast 1.02 to 1.07 push on the capture crop; paper lands at -1.2 degrees and settles to -0.4 degrees.

**Choreography:** capture PUSHES; sheet DROPS; hook words PUNCH; review line DRAWS; correction SLIDES; dossier SLAMS.

**Techniques:** captured-image compositing, SVG path drawing, per-word kinetic typography, CSS 3D paper transform.

**Depth:** BG blueprint grid + ghost identifier; MG capture crop + review sheet; FG redline + crop marks + dossier edge.

**SFX:** Short paper snap at the sheet landing; dry red-pencil scratch synchronized to the strike.

**Transition:** Vertical push starts at 2.35s and lasts 0.45s with `power3.inOut`. Outgoing content stays complete; the dossier sheet performs the exit. Beat 2's first spoken word begins at 2.54s inside the handoff.

## Beat 2: Open the Delivery File (2.54-7.30s)

**VO:** “En GoDigital, la solución se diseña alrededor del negocio: sistema, equipos e instalación.”

**Concept:** The film stops claiming and starts proving. A reconstructed anonymized case file opens, and its verified scope is checked against a real crop of the current delivery map.

**Visual:** Drafting paper fills the field. The dossier occupies the upper-left two-thirds with `CASO RESERVADO`, a black client redaction, `ENTREGA INTEGRAL`, and a compact scope register. The lower/right area carries a clipped `scroll-026.png` delivery map. A cobalt route connects the evidence labels `SISTEMA`, `EQUIPOS`, and `INSTALACIÓN`. The last check creates a second focal point against the dossier title.

**Camera:** Quick dive from 94% to 100% on the dossier, then a short lateral settle toward the route.

**Choreography:** dossier OPENS; identifier TYPES; redaction SWEEPS; rows CASCADE; route DRAWS; checks STAMP; status chip CLICKS.

**Techniques:** CSS 3D transforms, SVG path drawing, character typing, captured-image compositing.

**Depth:** BG paper texture + map crop; MG dossier + route; FG labels + status chip + registration ticks.

**SFX:** Three increasingly solid paper/check impacts on system, equipment, installation.

**Transition:** Blueprint shutter starts at 7.05s. The halves close over 0.25s, content swaps at exactly 7.30s on “Cada,” and the shutter opens over 0.25s.

## Beat 3: Inspect the Decision Log (7.30-12.34s)

**VO:** “Cada decisión queda clara. No es decoración. Es una solución completa para una operación real.”

**Concept:** The pace slows so the proof can be read. The actual decision outputs align into a calm graphite register, and the redline changes from correction into a bracket connecting the project logic.

**Visual:** Graphite owns the frame. A clipped strip from `scroll-052.png` sits as the midground register, reinforced with reconstructed rows for `NECESIDAD DOCUMENTADA`, `ALCANCE ENTENDIBLE`, `SOLUCIÓN OPERATIVA`, and `ENTREGA INSPECCIONABLE`. A small crop from `scroll-087.png` sits at the lower right as review evidence. A drafting-paper conclusion strip carries `NO ES DECORACIÓN` and `SOLUCIÓN COMPLETA` without becoming a centered title card.

**Camera:** Nearly still after the shutter, with only a 28px horizontal rule drift over the beat.

**Choreography:** register SLIDES; numbers DROP; rules DRAW; outputs TYPE; checks FILL; conclusion SETTLES; bracket DRAWS.

**Techniques:** character typing, SVG path drawing, structured image compositing, per-word typography.

**Depth:** BG ghost register + moving rule; MG decision rows + capture strip; FG paper conclusion + red bracket + checks.

**SFX:** Shutter closes with a graphite thump, then near-silence; a quiet pencil tick accents the final bracket.

**Transition:** Vertical push starts at 12.10s and lasts 0.45s with `power3.inOut`; the project-opening sheet rises and the CTA's first spoken word begins at 12.34s inside the handoff.

## Beat 4: Open the Conversation (12.34-15.00s)

**VO:** “Contanos qué necesitás ordenar por WhatsApp.”

**Concept:** The end card behaves like the first page of a real project file. The viewer receives one direct action and enough context to start without arriving with a predefined solution.

**Visual:** Blueprint Deep fills the canvas with evidence-bearing grid lines. A bright paper sheet occupies the center-left, while a smaller supporting sheet offsets to the right. The full GoDigital logo appears near the top. The main copy reads `CONTANOS QUÉ NECESITÁS ORDENAR`; the cobalt button says `HABLAR POR WHATSAPP`. Three short prompts check in below: what the business does, what work needs ordering, what must be left functioning. The verified short evidence line reads `wa.me/message/UFU3OSZAUAYKK1`. The red approval stamp lands in the lower safe area.

**Camera:** Fast sheet rise, then a stable final hold with a subtle shared shadow breath.

**Choreography:** sheet RISES; logo PAINTS; CTA words CASCADE; prompts CHECK; button PRESSES; arrow DRAWS; approval STAMP SLAMS.

**Techniques:** per-word kinetic typography, SVG draw/reveal, CSS 3D paper depth, clipped logo reveal.

**Depth:** BG blueprint grid + ghost case number; MG opening sheets + CTA; FG logo + arrow + stamp + registration ticks.

**SFX:** Crisp button-paper click, then one dry approval-stamp thunk on the final word.

**Final hold:** Narration ends at 14.56s. Keep the CTA and verified contact visible through 15.00s. No premature fade.

## Captions

- One conversational group at a time, 3-5 words, synchronized to `transcript.json`.
- Position around 620px from the bottom, adjusting upward where the dossier or CTA occupies the lower safe area.
- Archivo 800, 60-72px, maximum width 880px, maximum two lines.
- Default caption sheet uses `#FFFDF5` with `#182027`; evidence words use `#1746D1`; redline appears only on `NO ES DECORACIÓN`.
- Each group enters with a compact clip or directional slide, exits quickly, and receives a deterministic hard kill at its end time.
