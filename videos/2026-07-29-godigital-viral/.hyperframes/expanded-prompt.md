# Expanded Production Prompt: No es decoración. Es entrega.

## Style Lock

Create one decisive 15-second vertical social ad at 1080x1920 and 30 fps. Preserve GoDigital's Delivery Dossier world exactly: Cobalt Field `#1746D1`, Blueprint Deep `#08245F`, Drafting Paper Bright `#FFFDF5`, Drafting Paper `#F2ECD8`, Drafting Paper Muted `#DDD4B9`, Graphite `#182027`, Graphite Soft `#4C5760`, Redline `#A82F2A`, Blueprint Copy `#DBE4FF`, Blueprint Note `#B9CAFB`, Dark Field Copy `#CDD2D6`, Dark Field Copy Bright `#E6E9EB`, and Brand White `#FFFFFF`. Use Archivo 800 for display, Archivo 400/600 for supporting copy, and Azeret Mono 400/600 only for evidence and status. The material register is a senior project room: clipped paper edges, hard down-right sheet offsets, blueprint fields, redactions, checklists, tabs, rules, decision logs, and restrained stamps.

## Rhythm

`fast-fast-SLOW-fast`, expressed as `hook-PUNCH-hold-CTA`.

- Beat 1 disrupts immediately and lands all three hooks in the first second.
- Beat 2 accelerates through real delivery proof.
- Beat 3 slows down to crystallize the value and let “each decision is clear” register.
- Beat 4 regains momentum for a branded WhatsApp close and a memorable approval stamp.

## Global Rules

- Start inside the action. No fade from black and no neutral title card.
- Build every scene at its most visible hero frame before adding animation.
- Every scene fills the 1080x1920 frame with 8-10 purposeful elements across background, midground, and foreground.
- Every scene has two focal points and one authored ambient motion shared by its decoratives.
- Use only CSS/GSAP motion registered on one paused, synchronous HyperFrames timeline.
- No `Math.random()`, wall-clock animation, infinite repeats, asynchronous timeline creation, manual media playback, or iframes.
- Every scene uses distinct entrance choreography with at least three ease/direction combinations.
- No pre-transition element exits. The outgoing scene stays visually complete until the transition performs the handoff.
- Primary transition: editorial vertical push, used twice. Accent transition: mechanical blueprint shutter, used once at the value pivot.
- Captions use 3-5 word conversational groups, one group at a time, in the lower-middle safe area. Highlight only evidence-bearing words with cobalt, paper, or redline.
- Use the captured website as material: `scroll-000.png` for the opening/hero proof, `scroll-026.png` for the delivery map, `scroll-052.png` for the decision log, and `scroll-087.png` for the inspectable criteria.
- Logo appears in the first beat as a clipped masthead detail and in the final beat as the full closing mark.
- Sound is original/local: Spanish voiceover, dry paper movement, a graphite shutter hit, and a final approval-stamp thunk. No copyrighted music.

## Beat 1: Redline the Wrong Deliverable

**Concept:** The viewer lands in the middle of a project review. A polished drafting sheet says “WEB BONITA,” but a red pen instantly crosses it out while the narration rejects appearance without implementation. A real captured slice of the current GoDigital hero sits beneath the sheet, proving the film belongs to an actual website rather than a generic kinetic-type template.

**Mood:** Editorial intervention, decisive rather than glitchy. Think a creative director correcting the wrong brief in one confident stroke.

**Depth layers:**

- BG: cobalt blueprint grid, oversized low-contrast `ENTREGA / 001` ghost identifier, clipped strip from `capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png` with a slow vertical camera push.
- MG: bright drafting sheet with “WEB BONITA,” black redaction bar, and the first line of narration in oversized Archivo.
- FG: red review line, diagonal crop marks, small `REVISIÓN DE ALCANCE` label, logo fragment, paper tab, and frame registration ticks.

**Choreography:** Screenshot PUSHES slowly; sheet DROPS from above with physical weight; `WEB BONITA` PUNCHES on word by word; red line DRAWS across it; corrective phrase SLIDES from the left; dossier edge SLAMS up from below and becomes the transition carrier.

**Techniques:** image compositing with motion treatment, SVG path drawing, per-word kinetic typography, CSS 3D paper perspective.

**Transition out:** vertical push, about 0.42s with `power3.inOut`; the dossier page physically pushes the review sheet upward while Beat 2 rises from below.

## Beat 2: Open the Real Delivery File

**Concept:** The dossier opens at arm's length, then the camera dives close enough to read the evidence. The case remains anonymous, but the scope is concrete: system, tablet and thermal printer, installation, and a web tool. Checks land as the voice names system, equipment, and installation.

**Mood:** Rapid proof, operational and tactile. The energy comes from verified facts being processed, not from decorative noise.

**Depth layers:**

- BG: Drafting Paper with clipped portions of `scroll-026.png`, a cobalt side field, and a slow 1.5-degree paper drift.
- MG: reconstructed delivery dossier with `CASO RESERVADO`, redaction, `ENTREGA INTEGRAL`, scope rows, and three verified checklist entries.
- FG: cobalt status tab, connected SVG route, paper clips/crop marks, `SISTEMA`, `EQUIPOS`, `INSTALACIÓN` evidence labels, and a small `ENTREGADO` chip.

**Choreography:** Dossier OPENS from a 3D tilt; case identifier TYPES on; redaction bar SWEEPS across; scope rows CASCADE with a compact stagger; route line DRAWS; three checks STAMP in with different scales/rotations; status chip CLICKS into place.

**Techniques:** CSS 3D transforms, SVG path drawing, character typing, clipped capture compositing.

**Transition out:** blueprint shutter, about 0.52s total; top and bottom cobalt halves close like a scanner platen, swap while closed, then reopen on Beat 3.

## Beat 3: Make the Value Inspectable

**Concept:** Motion slows. The delivery map and decision log align into one calm register. “Cada decisión queda clara” is not a floating slogan: each word points to an actual documented output, turning the film's promise into an inspectable sequence.

**Mood:** Authoritative pause. A project room after the rapid review, with the logic laid flat on the table.

**Depth layers:**

- BG: Graphite field, large quiet `DECISIÓN / SALIDA` typography, clipped decision-log strip from `scroll-052.png`, and one slow horizontal registration-rule drift.
- MG: four-step decision register with `NECESIDAD DOCUMENTADA`, `ALCANCE ENTENDIBLE`, `SOLUCIÓN OPERATIVA`, and `ENTREGA INSPECCIONABLE`.
- FG: a drafting-paper conclusion strip, cobalt check marks, numbered tabs, one red bracket tying the outputs together, and a small cropped inspectable-criteria fragment from `scroll-087.png`.

**Choreography:** Log SLIDES into alignment; numbers DROP into their rows; rules DRAW left to right; outputs TYPE on in Azeret Mono; checks FILL with measured stagger; conclusion strip SETTLES and holds; red bracket DRAWS only after the register is readable.

**Techniques:** character typing, SVG path drawing, structured image compositing, per-word typography.

**Transition out:** vertical push, about 0.45s with `power3.inOut`; a bright project-opening sheet rises from the bottom and pushes the graphite register upward.

## Beat 4: Open the Conversation

**Concept:** The final page is a project-opening form, not a generic end card. It turns the dossier's reviewed evidence into one concrete next step: tell GoDigital what needs ordering. The WhatsApp action is unmistakable and the approval stamp makes the frame memorable.

**Mood:** Direct, warm, finished. High contrast and tactile confidence without hype.

**Depth layers:**

- BG: Blueprint Deep with evidence-bearing grid, one oversized `ABRIR PROYECTO` identifier, and a slow paper-shadow breath.
- MG: bright paper sheet with the CTA, three short intake prompts, and the verified WhatsApp URL rendered as a compact evidence line.
- FG: full GoDigital logo, white paper tab, cobalt CTA control with graphite offset, diagonal outbound arrow, red `LISTO PARA CONVERSAR` stamp, and a closing case number.

**Choreography:** Sheet RISES with the transition; logo PAINTS on through a clipped reveal; CTA words CASCADE quickly; three intake prompts CHECK in; button PRESSES forward; arrow DRAWS outward; final stamp SLAMS at the end of the narration and settles at less than three degrees rotation.

**Techniques:** per-word kinetic typography, SVG draw/reveal, CSS 3D paper depth, clipped logo reveal.

**Final resolve:** hold the complete frame long enough to read the CTA. Optional final 0.25s graphite dip only after the stamp has landed.

## Recurring Motifs

- Case identifier increments from `REVISIÓN / 001` to `ENTREGA / 001` to `DECISIÓN / 001` to `APERTURA / 001`.
- A single red review line evolves from rejection, to bracket, to approval stamp.
- Hard down-right paper offsets establish depth in every light-sheet frame.
- Cobalt grids appear only behind evidence, maps, or project-opening material.
- Caption emphasis uses the same evidence logic: red corrects, cobalt verifies, paper clarifies.

## Negative Prompt

No dark glassmorphism, neon glow, particles, random glitch, generic device mockups, glossy floating cards, bento grids, signal counters, fake analytics, fabricated dashboards, made-up results, client names, testimonials, awards, rainbow color, centered keynote layouts, decorative blueprint grids, or gratuitous parallax. Do not repeat “3 señales,” “THE DIFFERENCE,” or any old visual structure. Do not use red as a general accent. Do not let captions cover the key dossier evidence or CTA.
