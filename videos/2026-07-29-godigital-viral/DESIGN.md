# GoDigital Delivery Dossier Video Reference

## Overview

GoDigital uses an asymmetric editorial layout built from cobalt blueprint fields, warm drafting paper, graphite registers, redlines, tabs, checklists, and anonymized project sheets. Large Archivo headlines carry the proposition while Azeret Mono identifies evidence, status, and provenance. The visual world feels like a senior project room: precise, inspectable, tactile, and practical rather than glossy or promotional. Color owns meaningful regions, and real delivery artifacts carry the proof.

## Typography

- **Display / Headline**: Archivo 800. Tight tracking from `-0.035em` to `-0.03em`, line-height near `0.98`; oversized, direct, and left aligned.
- **Body / Controls**: Archivo 400 or 600. Clear, compact Spanish copy with comfortable line-height.
- **Evidence / Labels**: Azeret Mono 400 or 600. Uppercase, tracked identifiers, outputs, status, and provenance only.
- **Video Scale**: 96-160px display, 58-86px section titles, 34-52px body, 22-28px evidence labels on the 1080x1920 canvas.

## Elevation

Depth comes from physical paper overlap, clipped edges, tonal fields, and hard down-right offsets. Primary sheets use graphite or muted-paper offsets with a restrained ambient shadow; flat logs and registers use rules rather than shadows. Corners stay square or at `2px` for sheets, `8px` for controls, and pill geometry only for verified status. Sheet rotations remain below two degrees and stamp rotations below three degrees.

## Components

- **Anonymized Delivery Dossier**: warm paper sheet with case identifier, black redaction bar, definition-list facts, verified checklist, blue status chip, and red approval stamp.
- **Blueprint Delivery Map**: deep-blue technical field with grid, connected checkpoints, output labels, and one evidence-bearing redline note.
- **Need Sheet**: offset drafting-paper page with a red document tab, compact checklist, and budget margin note.
- **Exchange-Rate Workflow**: paper register connecting USD reference, daily rate, and bolivar operation with a confirmed-result note.
- **Decision Log**: graphite register with numbered stages, concrete outputs, and restrained check marks.
- **Project-Opening Sheet**: bright paper over blueprint deep, paired with a direct WhatsApp action and a short conversation checklist.
- **Document Transitions**: paper wipes, blueprint shutters, tab pulls, redline strikes, and stamp impacts; all transitions preserve the outgoing scene until the handoff.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use the real dossier, delivery map, decision log, and exchange-rate workflow as primary visual material.
- Let cobalt, paper, or graphite own whole frames; use red only to review, correct, or approve.
- Keep layouts asymmetric, editorial, and legible within vertical social safe areas.
- Animate sheets, rules, checks, tabs, redactions, and stamps as physical document work.
- Keep all claims tied to the captured website and anonymized approved case.

### Don'ts

- Do not use glassmorphism, neon glow, particles, random glitch, or generic tech-keynote styling.
- Do not use rounded card grids, floating glass panels, signal counters, or the previous “3 señales” structure.
- Do not fabricate metrics, testimonials, named clients, screenshots, awards, or outcomes.
- Do not use blueprint grids or monospace labels without evidence-bearing purpose.
- Do not scatter red as decoration or soften the system into generic cream editorial design.
