# Product Requirements Document (PRD): GoDigital Landing Page

## 1. Executive Summary
GoDigital is a boutique web and application design/development consultancy. The landing page must bridge the gap between high-end technical precision ("Obsidian Mesh" dark mode, "Fluid Tech" light mode) and a warm, human-centric service approach.

## 2. Core Value Propositions
- **Professional Integrity:** Strict adherence to deadlines and promises.
- **Warm Collaboration:** Clear, sincere explanations and empathetic client relations.
- **Strategic Planning:** Detailed budgeting and transparent service roadmaps.
- **Elite Craft:** High-quality designers and developers at competitive prices.

## 3. Target Audience
- Tech-savvy founders and startups looking for premium branding.
- Established businesses needing a modern digital overhaul.
- Clients who value both aesthetic excellence and technical reliability.

## 4. Key Page Sections
- **Hero:** Impactful value statement with the interconnected logo.
- **Services:** Web and App development details.
- **The "Boutique" Edge:** Highlighting the warm, sincere, and planned approach.
- **Quality Assurance:** Showcasing the elite designer/dev talent.
- **Process/Budgeting:** Transparent planning and competitive pricing.
- **Contact/CTA:** A streamlined inquiry form.

## 5. Visual Direction
- **Identity:** "Fluid Technology" (interconnected rounded typography).
- **Aesthetic:** iOS-style premium (glassmorphism, soft gradients, organic layers).
- **Themes:** Support for both Light (Fluid Tech) and Dark (Obsidian Mesh) modes.
